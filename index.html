About Zorvilla

Understanding Deepens Everything

Founded by composer and music theory faculty member Anneliese Voss, Zorvilla was built on a simple belief: musicians who understand harmony and structure play with more confidence and write with more freedom. Every student learns to hear and analyze, not just memorize.

  • ✓ Instructors with composition and academic backgrounds
  • ✓ Ear training built into every single lesson
  • ✓ Programs for curious hobbyists through exam candidates
  • ✓ Original composition guided from first sketch to finished score

Our Programs

A Program For Every Musician

From your first scale degree to your first finished score — every program deepens how you actually hear and understand music.

📐
